"A Numerical Investigation of Scalar Inflation Fields in the Standard Model of Cosmology" is my Undergraduate dissertation supervised by Dr. David Mulryne in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of BSc Physics from Queen Mary University of London, which I received in 2023 with First Class Honours.
It begins by recalling Einstein's Field Equations and how they replaced the flat and unchanging description of space and time given by the classical theory of Newtonian mechanics with the warping and responsive 4-dimensional space-time manifold given by the relativistic theory.
We recall Alexander Friedmann's cosmological solution to them, and the intuition behind the cosmological assumptions that assume the universe appears as a homogeneous and uniform fluid at the largest scales.
